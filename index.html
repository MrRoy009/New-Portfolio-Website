I don't chase
backtests. I try to beat break them.

Every strategy I write has to survive pre-registered validation before it is allowed to send a signal: walk-forward tests, cost and delay stress, a placebo benchmark, block bootstrap, Deflated Sharpe and PBO. So far none has passed, and the repos say exactly why. I think a negative result written up honestly is worth more than a pretty equity curve.
